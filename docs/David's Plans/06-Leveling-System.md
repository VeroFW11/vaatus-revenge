## My notes

**Campfires:**
- Enemy aggro disappears.
- Replenish (health, and whatever else refills).
- **Level up using runes.**

You grind to get stronger.

**Healing item: spirit water from the Spirit Oasis.**

**From the planning session with Jeremy (27 Sep, [transcript](../Transcribed%20Notes/2026-09-27-Planning-Session-Bending-and-Elden-Ring.md)). How Elden Ring does it:**
- Runes come from **killing enemies, selling items, and picking them up** in the world.
- Selling items earns very little. **Farming enemies** is the efficient way to earn (e.g. the famous farm near Mohg, Lord of Blood).
- When you die, all your runes stay where you fell. **Die again before getting back and they're gone for good.** You can't bank them.
- One of us **didn't like that**; the other said it makes you think about when to spend.
- **Levelling gets much harder late in the game**, because costs climb steeply.

## Claude's suggestions

**Two separate progression tracks work well:**

1. **Levels (grind):** runes from enemies → spend at campfires → +1 stat each time. Level cost rises gently.
2. **Story unlocks (can't be ground):** masters teach new elements, and story moments unlock Avatar State stages.

That way, grinding makes you stronger but can't skip the story, and the story always feels like a big power jump.

### Spirit water

**Lore:** the Spirit Oasis at the North Pole is home to Tui and La, the Moon and Ocean spirits. Its water has special healing power; Katara used a vial of it to bring Aang back to life. That makes it a strong, canon-true healing item.

**How it could work, based on Elden Ring's flasks:**
- You carry a **vial** with a few charges, and they **refill at every campfire**.
- **More charges:** find extra vials hidden around the world (Elden Ring uses Golden Seeds for this).
- **Stronger healing:** find **moon drops** or offerings to Tui and La (Elden Ring uses Sacred Tears).
- **A pilgrimage:** travelling to the Spirit Oasis itself could permanently upgrade your vials. That rewards exploring and ties into the Water region.
