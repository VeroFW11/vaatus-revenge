# 06 · Leveling System

## My notes

**Campfires:**
- Enemy aggro disappears.
- Replenish (health, and whatever else refills).
- **Level up using runes.**

You grind to get stronger.

## Things to decide

- [ ] **What are "runes" called in our world?** Keep "runes", or something from Avatar (spirit energy, chi, spirit lights)?
- [ ] **What do you lose when you die?** In Elden Ring you drop all runes where you died, and dying again before getting them back loses them for good.
- [ ] **What refills at a campfire?** Health, chi/bending, healing items?
- [ ] **Do enemies respawn when you rest?** In Elden Ring they do, which is what makes grinding possible.
- [ ] **What's the healing item?** Elden Ring has flasks. Ours could be healing water, spirit water from the Spirit Oasis, or tea.
- [ ] **How do levels and skills relate?** Levelling raises stats; how do you get skill points (see [05](05-Stats-and-Skill-Trees.md))?
- [ ] **How does the Avatar State unlock?** Through levelling, through story, or both? (Your story notes say both.)

## Claude's suggestions

**Two separate progression tracks work well:**

1. **Levels (grind):** runes from enemies → spend at campfires → +1 stat each time. Level cost rises gently.
2. **Story unlocks (can't be ground):** masters teach new elements, and story moments unlock Avatar State stages.

That way, grinding makes you stronger but can't skip the story, and the story always feels like a big power jump.
