## Things to decide

- [ ] **How do weapons and bending fit together?** In the show most benders fight without weapons. Options: weapons and bending are separate move sets, weapons boost bending (a fan for air, a staff for water), or weapons are for non-bending moves only.
- [ ] **Weapon types.** Fill in the list below.
- [ ] **Armour.** Pieces (head, chest, arms, legs) like Elden Ring, or full outfits? Does armour weight slow you down?
- [ ] **Upgrades.** Can you upgrade weapons (Elden Ring uses smithing stones)? With what?
- [ ] **Status effects.** Which ones? (See the list below.)
- [ ] **Stat requirements.** Can you use any weapon, or do some need certain stats?

## Claude's suggestions

**Weapon types from the show:**

| Weapon | Seen with | Suggested scaling |
|---|---|---|
| Dual dao swords | Zuko (Blue Spirit) | Dexterity |
| War fans | Kyoshi Warriors | Dexterity / Air |
| Staff / glider | Aang | Dexterity / Air |
| Sword | Sokka (space sword), Piandao | Strength / Dexterity |
| Boomerang | Sokka | Dexterity (ranged) |
| Club / hammer | Earth Kingdom soldiers | Strength |
| Spear | Fire Nation soldiers | Strength |
| Chi-blocking gloves | Ty Lee | Dexterity, stuns bending |

**Possible status effects:** Burn (fire), Freeze/Slow (water), Stagger/Crush (earth), Knockback/Off-balance (air), **Chi-blocked** (enemy can't bend for a while), **Spirit corruption** (Vaatu's version of Elden Ring's scarlet rot).
