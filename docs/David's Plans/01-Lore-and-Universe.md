# 01 · Lore and Universe

## My notes

- The Spirit World and the real world have clashed, and it's the Avatar's job to restore balance.
- **The player is a new, original Avatar, not Korra.**
- Leaning towards **leaving Aang out**, so the story centres on the new character (not final).
- Characters who appear:
  - ~~Aang~~ (probably out)
  - Kyoshi
  - Wan
  - Raava
  - Vaatu
  - Roku

## Things to decide

- [ ] **When is the game set?** A new Avatar has to fit somewhere in the Avatar cycle. The options are below under "Timeline options".
- [ ] **Starting element vs. the Avatar cycle.** In canon the Avatar is always reborn in the next nation in the order Air → Water → Earth → Fire, which clashes with letting the player choose any starting element. How do we explain it?
- [ ] **How do past Avatars appear?** As visions at campfires, as spirit guides, as mentors who teach techniques, or as memories you unlock?
- [ ] **Why has the clash happened now?** What broke the balance, and why is Vaatu back?
- [ ] **Who is the player character?** A fixed Avatar with their own name and backstory, or a custom character?
- [ ] **How faithful to the show do we stay?** Canon-accurate, or free to change lore where it makes a better game?

## Claude's suggestions

**Show lore that fits the premise:**
- Raava (light, order) and Vaatu (darkness, chaos) are ancient spirits locked in a cycle. Wan, the first Avatar, fused with Raava and trapped Vaatu in the Tree of Time for 10,000 years.
- In *Legend of Korra* Book 2, Vaatu escapes during Harmonic Convergence and is defeated. But the show says he can never be destroyed for good: as long as there's darkness in people's hearts, Vaatu can return. That's a ready-made reason for "Vaatu's Revenge".
- Korra left the spirit portals open at the end of Book 2, so spirits and humans now live side by side. A world where those two worlds have "clashed" follows naturally from that.

**Possible framing:** the player wakes with their memories broken, the same way Raava was broken when Vaatu nearly destroyed her. Recovering past lives (e.g. Wan → Kyoshi → Roku) is how you rebuild the Avatar State. That links the lore directly to progression.

### Timeline options

The known chain of Avatars goes … Szeto → Yangchen → Kuruk → Kyoshi → Roku → Aang → Korra. There's no gap between named Avatars, so a new Avatar has to sit before, after or outside that chain.

| Option | Where it fits | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. The Avatar after Korra** | Future, after Korra dies | Kyoshi, Roku and Wan are all past lives. Vaatu's "revenge" is literal: he comes back for the Avatar line that beat him. The spirit portals Korra opened already make the two worlds clash. | Korra's era had cars, cities and radio. We'd need a reason the world feels ancient again, e.g. the spirit clash has overgrown and ruined the modern world, like Elden Ring's shattered world. |
| **B. An ancient Avatar** | Far past, between Wan and Szeto | Pure fantasy setting, total creative freedom, few canon constraints | Kyoshi and Roku haven't been born yet, so they can't appear. Only Wan, Raava and Vaatu are available. |
| **C. Alternate timeline** | A "what if" version of the world | Use any characters you like and ignore canon | Loses the anchor of real canon, and fans may find it harder to place |

**How A solves the starting element problem:** the Spirit World clash has **broken the Avatar cycle**, so the new Avatar could be born in any nation. That becomes a sign from the very first scene that something is wrong with the world.

**Aang doesn't have to disappear completely** for the player to be a new character. Under A he'd still be a past life. He could be the one past life who stays silent, which could itself be a mystery, or he could simply not be one of the lives you meet.
