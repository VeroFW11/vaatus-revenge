# 09 · Boss System (Bosses and Enemies)

## My notes

- **Corrupted spirits are the bosses and the enemies.**
- **Bosses and enemies also use every type of martial art** from the [Martial arts in the World of Avatar](https://avatar.fandom.com/wiki/Martial_arts_in_the_World_of_Avatar) page, used against the player:
  - **Armed combat:** bending, technology, weaponry
  - **Unarmed combat:** chi-blocking, hand-to-hand
  - (and the page's **hybrid styles**: bending combined with weapons)
- **Vaatu is the final boss.** He's insanely strong, and you need all four elements plus a mastered Avatar State to fight him.
- **Decision: the Vaatu fight happens in the Spirit World at the Tree of Time**, while he tries to break out early. You **reseal** him rather than destroy him, which keeps canon intact (see [01](01-Lore-and-Universe.md)).

### Human enemy roster by martial art type

This follows the categories on the wiki page and is checked against our ancient era.

**Armed combat: bending**
| Enemy | Style | Notes |
|---|---|---|

**Armed combat: technology** ⚠️ lore check
The page says tech-based combat has **a long tradition, but grew in importance in modern times**. The famous examples (electrified gloves, gas devices, mecha tanks) are **Korra-era Equalist technology**, which breaks our era.
Lore-safe ancient tech: **traps, nets and bolas, chains, repeating crossbows** (which were real in ancient China), **siege engines, smoke from alchemy, and bladed contraptions**.

### Boss idea: the style-switcher

A human boss who changes **martial art each phase**, e.g. phase 1 sword master, phase 2 chi-blocker, phase 3 firebender. It tests whether you've learned to read each style. The page backs this up with its "hybrid styles" section, which describes masters who blend many styles, as Kyoshi did.

**Lore check:** Unalaq and the Dark Avatar come from Korra's era, thousands of years after ours, so they can't appear. Vaatu himself stays sealed in the Tree of Time until the final fight (see [01](01-Lore-and-Universe.md)).

**What makes a souls-like boss good:** it's hard but fair. Each attack has a clear tell (wind-up), the player can learn patterns, and every death teaches something. Jeremy will have strong opinions here.
