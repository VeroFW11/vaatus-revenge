# 10 · Combat and Martial Arts

## My notes

- **Attention to detail** in the map and fighting mechanics.
- **The anime used real martial arts styles, and so should we.**

## Things to decide

- [ ] **How realistic is the animation?** Motion-capture reference, reference videos, or Mixamo animations to start with?
- [ ] **Combat basics.** Light attack, heavy attack, dodge, block/parry, lock-on, like Elden Ring?
- [ ] **Switching elements mid-fight.** Once you know more than one element, how do you switch? A stance button, element wheel, or combos that mix elements?
- [ ] **Avatar State in combat.** A temporary power mode with a meter, like a super move?

## Claude's suggestions

**The real martial arts behind each bending style (from the show's production):**

| Element | Martial art | Feel |
|---|---|---|
| Water | **Tai Chi** | Slow, circular, redirecting force |
| Earth | **Hung Gar** | Rooted stances, strong blocks, powerful strikes |
| Toph's earthbending | **Southern Praying Mantis** | Close-range, reactive |
| Fire | **Northern Shaolin** | Fast, aggressive, long-range strikes and kicks |
| Air | **Baguazhang** | Circle walking, evasion, constant movement |

The show's martial arts consultant was **Sifu Kisu**. Collecting reference videos of each style for the animator is a good early task, and it belongs in `docs/knowledge/`.
