# 02 · Storyline

## My notes

- You wake up as the Avatar and the world is in chaos between the Spirit World and the real world.
- Your mission is to defeat Vaatu.
- At the start you **can't access the Avatar State**.
- As you level up, learn moves and progress through the story, you unlock more parts of the Avatar State and grow more powerful.
- You must **learn all four elements** and **master the Avatar State** before you can fight Vaatu. He's insanely strong.
- **Vaatu is the final boss.**

## Things to decide

- [ ] Opening scene: where do you wake up, and what's the first thing you see?
- [ ] What order are the elements learned in? Is it fixed or player-chosen? (Your starting element is first; see [03](03-Starting-Classes.md).)
- [ ] How many Avatar State stages are there, and which story moment unlocks each one?
- [ ] Who teaches each element? A master in each region, a past Avatar, or both?
- [ ] Does Vaatu appear or taunt you before the final fight?
- [ ] Is there one ending or several? (Elden Ring has several endings based on player choices.)
- [ ] How much story is told directly versus found in item descriptions and the environment? (Your gameplay notes say "nothing ever really gets explained".)

## Claude's suggestions

**A draft story skeleton to react to, not a decision:**

| Act | Beat | Avatar State stage |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Wake up in your home region and learn your starting element. The world is overrun with corrupted spirits. | None: locked |
| 2 | Travel to the other regions. Each has a master to find, a corrupted spirit ruling it, and a past-life artifact. | Stage 1: glowing eyes, short power burst |
| 3 | After three elements, cross into the Spirit World and face your past lives. | Stage 2: sustained Avatar State |
| 4 | Master the final element, reconnect with Raava, climb to Vaatu. | Stage 3: full mastery |
| Final | Vaatu | Everything |
