# 08 · Artifacts and Loot

## My notes

- You can find **super powerful artifacts** left by benders, and even by the **Avatar's past lives**.
- The game rewards you well for exploring: Avatar artifacts left from past lives.
- Higher-tier loot: weapons, armour, practical items, combo loot with status effects.
- **Almost nothing is meaningless.**

## Things to decide

- [ ] **What do artifacts do?** Unique abilities, stat boosts, Avatar State upgrades, or unlocking memories of past lives?
- [ ] **How many artifacts per past Avatar?** One signature item each, or a full set?
- [ ] **Loot tiers.** How many tiers, and what are they called?
- [ ] **Where does loot come from?** Chests, bosses, hidden areas, masters, side quests?
- [ ] **Lore on items.** Elden Ring tells much of its story through item descriptions. Do we? (That fits "nothing gets explained".)
- [ ] **Are there sets?** E.g. wearing all of Kyoshi's items gives a bonus.

## Claude's suggestions

**Past-life artifact ideas.** Lore check: the game is set before Kyoshi, Roku, Aang, Kuruk and Yangchen were born, so their items can't exist yet (see [01](01-Lore-and-Universe.md)).

| Past life | Artifact ideas |
|---|---|
| Wan | Something linked to Raava, the lion turtles or the first Harmonic Convergence; maybe the key to the final Avatar State stage |
| Original ancient Avatars | One signature item per past life we invent. We write each one's story and let the item hint at it. |
| Lion turtles | In canon they granted people bending before the Avatar existed; relics from them could be among the oldest, most powerful items |
| Ancient benders | Items from the first benders who learned from the original sources: the Moon (water), badgermoles (earth), dragons (fire) and sky bison (air) |

**Loot tier sketch:** Common → Crafted → Master's → Legendary → Avatar Relic.

**Design tip:** "almost nothing is meaningless" means every chest needs a reason to exist. A good test: if a player finds this item, does it change what they do next?
