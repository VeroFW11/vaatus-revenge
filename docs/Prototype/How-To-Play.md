# How to play: combos in plain words

A one-page guide for the combat sandbox. Xbox buttons. The fastest way to learn is the tutorial: press **View** (the small button left of the Xbox button) in the sandbox, or use **Vaatu's Revenge ▸ Play Combat Tutorial** in Unity. In the game, **F1** shows all of this too.

## The four things that make a combo

1. **Press X as each hit lands.** Every element has a five-hit string on X. Watch for the flash and the gold ring: press as the hit connects and the next hit comes out faster and harder. Mashing still works, just slower. Keep the beat for all five and the last hit knocks them up.
2. **Wait for a different ending.** X, X, then a short breath (about half a second), then X. That's the **pause finisher**: a different last move.
3. **Dodging doesn't break your combo.** Tap B. Push the stick toward the enemy and you slip in close; push away and you hop back; push sideways and you circle. If you were on hit 3, your next X is hit 4. Press **X near the end of a dodge** and you dash back in with a counter (the **dodge strike**), which counts as the next hit.
4. **Switch element mid-string.** Hold **RB** and press a face button. **The button's colour is the element:**

| Hold RB + | Element | Plays like |
|---|---|---|
| **B** (red) | Fire | Fast and steady. Hits on the beat build Momentum (more damage) |
| **X** (blue) | Water | Slower and forgiving. Long reach, pulls them in, heals you, easiest parry |
| **A** (green) | Earth | Slow and heavy. Hits through their attacks. Hold LB to block |
| **Y** (yellow) | Air | Fastest. Lots of little hits, circles round them, longest dodge |

Do it **in the middle of a string** and the string carries on in the new element from the same hit. That switching hit lands a bit harder.

## MIX: why you mix elements

Every element that **lands a hit** in one combo adds to MIX (shown next to the hit counter). It counts for the whole combo, across strings, until the hit counter runs out or you get hit. With this many elements landed **before** a finisher:

- **2 elements:** every hit is stronger and the finisher hits harder.
- **3 elements:** the finisher throws them in the air.
- **4 elements:** the finisher breaks their guard (they stagger).

Switching on its own earns nothing: the new element has to land a hit. Switching has a short cooldown, so put at least one X between two switches.

## Other buttons

| Button | Does |
|---|---|
| Hold X | Launcher: throws the enemy up and you jump after them. Then X X X in the air |
| Y | Zip to a far enemy (keeps your combo going) |
| A | Jump |
| Tap LB | Parry (just before a hit lands). Earth: hold LB to block |
| Tap RB | Ranged attack of your element |
| Hold LB + X / Y / B | Charged heavy (let go in the gold band) / mid-range move / close all-round move |
| D-pad down | Heal |

**The mark above your head** is danger sense: an enemy is about to hit you. **Gold** = you can parry it (LB). **Red** = dodge it (B). Press when it **turns white**.

## Try this

- **Beat practice:** X X X X X, pressing as each hit lands. You'll hear it speed up.
- **Fire into Water:** X X, RB + X, X X. Two Fire hits, then three Water hits ending on Water's finisher.
- **Dodge and carry on:** X X, B away, X. The X is a dodge strike that dashes back in as hit 3.
- **Pause finisher, Fire:** X X (wait) X X: a sweeping kick into a rising kick that launches.
- **Launcher and juggle:** hold X, then X X X in the air.
- **Three-element launch:** X, RB + X (Water), X, RB + A (Earth), X. Fire, Water and Earth have all landed, so Earth's finisher throws them up.
- **The full MIX:** the three-element launch, then straight into a second string with Air in it: X, RB + Y (Air), X X X. Four elements in one combo: that finisher breaks their guard.
- **Graduation (the tutorial's last step):** a 20-hit combo using three elements. Keep the beat, switch mid-string, dodge when the mark flashes.

Keyboard: X is the left mouse button, B is Left Shift, A is Space, Y is F, LB is Q, RB is the right mouse button. Elements are **1** Fire, **2** Water, **3** Earth, **4** Air. **F7** starts or quits the tutorial, **F8** skips a step.
