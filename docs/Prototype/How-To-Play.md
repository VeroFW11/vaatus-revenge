# How to play: combos in plain words

A one-page guide for the combat sandbox. Xbox buttons. The fastest way to learn is the tutorial: press **View** (the small button left of the Xbox button) in the sandbox, or use **Vaatu's Revenge ▸ Play Combat Tutorial** in Unity. In the game, **F1** shows all of this too.

## The four things that make a combo

1. **Press X so it lands with each hit.** Every element has a five-hit string on X. Gold rings close on a circle (under the hit counter, and on the floor at your feet): press each time a ring touches, so your press lands *with* the hit. The ring for the next hit is already on its way (fainter, further out) while the current one closes, so you can see the rhythm coming instead of reacting to it; only the very first press after the opening X comes quickly. Don't wait for the flash and the chime: they only confirm a hit on the beat, and reacting to them is too late. On the beat, the next hit comes out faster and harder. Mashing still works, but it's a little slower and weaker, and it burns stamina so fast your string stalls. On Fluid, keep the beat for all five and the last hit knocks them up.
2. **Wait for a different ending.** X, X, then wait until your hands are back in guard and **the circle glows blue**, then X X. That's the **pause finisher**: a different two-hit ending. (The wait is a little different in each element, so go by the blue circle, not by counting. On Punishing the blue lasts only about a quarter of a second: press as it turns blue.)
3. **Dodging doesn't break your combo.** Tap B. Push the stick toward the enemy and you slip in close; push away and you hop back; push sideways and you circle. If you were on hit 3, your next X is hit 4. Press **X near the end of a dodge** and you dash back in with a counter (the **dodge strike**), which counts as the next hit.
4. **Switch element mid-string.** Hold **RB** and press a face button. **The button's colour is the element:**

| Hold RB + | Element | Plays like |
|---|---|---|
| **B** (red) | Fire | Fast and steady. Hits on the beat build Momentum (more damage) |
| **X** (blue) | Water | Slower and forgiving. Long reach, pulls them in, heals you, easiest parry |
| **A** (green) | Earth | Slow and heavy. Hits through their attacks. Hold LB to block |
| **Y** (yellow) | Air | Fastest. Lots of little hits, circles round them, longest dodge (on Fluid) |

Do it **in the middle of a string** and the string carries on in the new element with its next hit (X X, RB + X is hit 3 in Water). That switching hit lands a bit harder. **Let go of RB after switching:** while RB is held, every face button picks an element (B would switch to Fire instead of dodging). If you do keep RB held for the next X while already in Water, it simply carries on in Water.

## MIX: why you mix elements

Every element that **lands a hit** in one combo adds to MIX (shown next to the hit counter). It counts for the whole combo, across strings, until the hit counter runs out or you get hit. With this many elements landed **before** a finisher:

- **2 elements:** every hit is stronger and the finisher hits harder.
- **3 elements:** the finisher throws them in the air.
- **4 elements:** the finisher launches them too and tops up Fire's Momentum. A foe too heavy to launch has its guard broken instead (it staggers).

Switching on its own earns nothing: the new element has to land a hit. Switching has a short cooldown, so put at least one X between two switches (on Punishing, two). If a switch doesn't happen, the element wheel shakes and its cooldown dots flash amber: the X still counts, in the element you were in.

## Other buttons

| Button | Does |
|---|---|
| Press and keep holding X | That hit is followed by the launcher (at any point in a string): it throws the enemy up and you jump after them. Then X X X in the air |
| Y | Zip to a far enemy (keeps your combo going) |
| A | Jump |
| Tap LB | Parry (just before a hit lands). Earth: hold LB to block |
| Tap RB | Ranged attack of your element |
| Hold LB + X / Y / B | Charged heavy (let go in the gold band) / mid-range move / close all-round move |
| D-pad down | Heal |
| Menu | Pause. On the pause screen, Y pages through the combos and controls (the F1 pages) |

**The mark above your head** is danger sense: an enemy is about to hit you. **Gold** = you can parry it (LB). **Red** = dodge it (B). On Fluid it **turns white** at the moment to press; on Punishing there is no white flash, so read the swing.

## Try this

- **Beat practice:** X X X X X, pressing each time a gold ring touches the circle. You'll hear it speed up.
- **Fire into Water:** X X, RB + X, X X. Two Fire hits, then three Water hits ending on Water's finisher.
- **Dodge and carry on:** X X, B away, X. The X is a dodge strike that dashes back in as hit 3. (On Punishing a dodge can't be cut short and X is only remembered for a moment, so press X near the end of the dodge, not at its start.)
- **Pause finisher, Fire:** X X (wait for the blue circle) X X: a sweeping kick into a rising kick that launches.
- **Launcher and juggle:** press and hold X, then X X X in the air.
- **Three-element launch:** X, RB + X (Water), X, RB + A (Earth), X. Fire, Water and Earth have all landed, so Earth's finisher throws them up. (On Punishing the switch cooldown is longer, so spread it over two strings: X X, RB + X, X X, then X X, RB + A, X X. Fire and Water land in the first string and Earth before the second string's finisher, so that one launches. On Punishing a single string only fits two elements before its finisher.)
- **The full MIX:** the three-element launch, then straight into a second string with Air in it: X, RB + Y (Air), X X X. Four elements in one combo: that finisher launches them and tops up Fire's Momentum.
- **Graduation (the tutorial's last step):** a 20-hit combo using three elements. Keep the beat and switch mid-string; dodge whenever you need to, the combo survives it.

Keyboard: X is the left mouse button, B is Left Shift, A is Space, Y is F, LB is Q, RB (tap, the ranged attack) is the right mouse button. To switch element on the keyboard use **1** Fire, **2** Water, **3** Earth, **4** Air. **F7** starts or quits the tutorial, **F8** skips a step.
